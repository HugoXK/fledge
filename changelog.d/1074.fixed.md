A text field on `/settings` now keeps the same 44px height as the select beside it. The field's own box was 43.7px tall — `padding: 10px 12px` plus a 14px line box lands just under the touch-target floor — so the measured visual gate reported `input is 736x44px, under the 44px touch floor` on every desktop width in both colour schemes and both languages. The floor now sits on the field itself rather than on a wrapper, so a field that is not inside a label cannot fall short again; checkbox and radio glyphs keep their small size and keep reaching the floor through their label.
