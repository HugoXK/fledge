The nginx sample now sends `Strict-Transport-Security`, `X-Content-Type-Options`, `Referrer-Policy` and `X-Frame-Options` with `always` on both server blocks, so a self-hoster who keeps the documented default `BASE_URL` still gets HSTS and nginx's own 301/502 responses carry the framing and sniffing headers. `deploy-check` now refuses a rendered sample that drops one of them or omits `always`.
