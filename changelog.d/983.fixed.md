A refused or conflicting rule override keeps the text and revision the maintainer submitted. The page used to answer with a redirect and re-render the textarea from the stored row, so a submission refused for its size, an invalid character, a bad revision or a lost revision race was thrown away; it now re-renders in place with the submitted text above the banner (and, for a path outside the three rule files, in a read-only box), so the maintainer can trim it and resubmit without retyping.
