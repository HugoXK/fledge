A repository's schedule now has a console at `/repos/:id/schedule`: it shows the stored schedule (a five-field cron with its IANA zone, or a fixed interval), the next run from the same planner the worker uses, the newest decided instant with its state, reason, missed count and attempt count, whether a live lease holds it, and the last instant whose job succeeded. A maintainer can save or delete the schedule from that page, which is the first product surface that calls the schedule store at all; a value nobody recorded reads `unknown` rather than zero.
