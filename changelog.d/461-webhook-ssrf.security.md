Outbound notification webhooks now resolve their host and refuse loopback, RFC1918, link-local, multicast, cloud-metadata, IPv6 loopback/ULA and IPv4-mapped private targets before sending the alert body. Redirects are re-checked one hop at a time, production refuses to start with an unsafe webhook, and the optional `NOTIFY_WEBHOOK_ALLOWED_HOSTS` list lets a self-hosted deployment trust an internal target.
