Plan approval is a decision now: a completed plan opens with one primary **Approve and run**, a **Reject with a reason** and an **Ask for a revised plan** that queues a new plan instead of editing the decided one, and the decision itself is recorded on the job as the plan fingerprint, the base sha, the actor, the allow/deny paths and the time. Both the job page and `/fledge approve` write that same record, so a second decision is refused and keeps the first one standing, an edit made after the page was opened is refused instead of approved, and a plan whose base moved since it finished says which two shas it is between. A plan without structured evidence renders as incomplete with the primary disabled and the reason beside it.
