The schedule console now shows what an occurrence was decided under. Beside the live facts, a "Frozen at claim" card prints the configuration and authorization the newest decided instant recorded — kind, expression or interval, zone, window, owner, installation, permission and role — and names the fields a later edit moved. A row that recorded nothing prints unknown for every field instead of echoing today's schedule, so "what did this run as" has an answer even after the schedule or the repository permission changes; a snapshot whose own timing revision is not the row's is marked as a mismatch rather than printed as if it belonged.
