The queue-wait and job-duration histograms now keep bucket boundaries up to one hour, so the p95 alerts that compare against ten and thirty minutes can actually fire. Before this the highest finite boundary was five minutes, and `histogram_quantile` never interpolates inside the `+Inf` bucket: a p95 that landed there read as five minutes, so both rules were unreachable rather than merely imprecise. The thresholds themselves are unchanged.
