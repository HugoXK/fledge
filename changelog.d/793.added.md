The job page's "Why this change" block now carries the rest of the story before a maintainer decides: the alternatives that were considered, what remains uncertain or is accepted as residual risk, the explicit list of what was not run, and the model and role that did the work. A pull request body that does not carry one of those sections shows `not stated` instead of dropping the row, and the model/role row is read from the job record, not from the body.
