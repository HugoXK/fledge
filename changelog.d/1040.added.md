Job pages now show whether the red test a job claims failed for the intended reason. When the agent reports the base run, Fledge classifies it and shows the verdict (`red`, `unverified` or `passed`), the signature behind it, the reason and a digest of the output beside the agent's sentence; a run taken on another tree is marked as a base mismatch. A maintainer can confirm the claim or challenge it with a reason, and the decision, its author and its time stay on the job page.
