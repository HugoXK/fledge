Added a schedule preview: the console page at /schedule and GET /api/schedule/preview show the next runs of a cron expression or a fixed interval, with each run in the schedule's IANA zone. A local time removed by a daylight-saving change is shown as skipped, not shifted, and a refusal names the field that is wrong. The interval form has no timezone, and an interval submitted with one is refused rather than shown as if a zone applied.
