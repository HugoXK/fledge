Feedback and unexpected-error reports now go to the server FlyBot's Fledge Lead through a repo already connected to Fledge (`FLEDGE_LEAD_REPO_ID`) or a direct bridge (`FLEDGE_LEAD_BRIDGE_URL/TOKEN/SESSION_ID`). A per-ref signed triage token, expiring after seven days, with a bounded five-attempt delivery outbox, lets the Lead read the redacted record and write a user-facing decision back through the triage API; statuses carry their required message/issue/PR/version fields and a retry is a no-op; the reporter gets an in-app and email update for a user problem, a duplicate, a confirmed issue, a fix with its issue/PR and version, and a final “now live” notice when the running version catches up. The Lead gets a ready-to-use sanitized issue draft; a public GitHub issue may @ the reporter only after a separate sanitized-publication consent, and no raw message, stack, email or token is published by Fledge. Settings also lists the reporter's own feedback status, links and fixed/released version.
