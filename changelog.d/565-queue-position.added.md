Queued jobs now show their position in the instance queue and the queue length (`#3/7`) together with the priority they compete on and whether it came from a schedule, a manual run or a follow-up. The job page lists the viewer's own queued jobs that are ahead, with each one's repository, priority and arrival, and states how many other accounts' queued jobs are ahead without naming any of them. The order is priority, then arrival — the same two keys the claimer uses — so equal jobs share a position, and the number is the order, not a promise that the job starts next.
