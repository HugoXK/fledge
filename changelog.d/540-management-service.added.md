Cancelling a job from its page is a replay-safe command now: the form carries the revision it rendered and an idempotency key, so a double submit returns the same receipt instead of acting twice, and a page that went out of date after someone else's write is refused with a 管理变更冲突 / revision conflict message instead of cancelling a job the page never saw. Every cancel also leaves a receipt (who, which channel, target, before/after revision, result) next to its audit row.
