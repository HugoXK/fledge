Fledge now reconciles stored team session IDs with the live DSH state after a health probe and immediately before dispatch. A DSH restart that creates new Chief/Lead/Product/Test/Security/Reviewer/Dev sessions updates `team_json` and `chief_session` before the next job, so a job is never forwarded to a dead session and reaped as stalled. The refresh is audited and leaves the stored team untouched if any role is missing.
