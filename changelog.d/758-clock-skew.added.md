`GET /readyz` now carries `clock.skewSeconds` — the offset between the process clock and GitHub's `Date` header that the GitHub App client already measures for its JWT — and `/metrics` publishes it as `fledge_clock_skew_seconds`, with a `FledgeClockSkewHigh` alert at two minutes of drift. When the measurement fails or no App is configured the field is `null` and the metric series is absent rather than `0`, so an unmeasured clock never reads as one that is in sync. The clock itself is not changed: no stored timestamp is shifted and `nowIso()` is untouched.
