Backup and restore-drill health now has a machine-readable surface: `/metrics` publishes `fledge_backup_age_hours`, `fledge_backup_copies`, `fledge_backup_encrypted_copies`, `fledge_restore_drill_age_hours` and `fledge_restore_drill_ok`, and two new Prometheus rules page when the newest copy is older than the expected interval or the last drill failed or aged out. A stale copy or a failed drill also raises one in-app ops alert, deduplicated by the existing `OPS_ALERT_COOLDOWN_MINUTES`, so a night that stopped backing up no longer waits for somebody to open the admin page.
