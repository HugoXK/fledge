A repository's Delivery tab now shows what happened after each merge: whether the change was reverted and when, follow-up issues that point at the pull request, failing runs on the default branch at the merge commit, and the cost of each kept change. The numbers are read from a post-merge ledger with one row per merged pull request; a fact nobody recorded shows — rather than 0, a single delivery is labelled as a sample, and the ledger is deleted on the same 365-day window as the jobs that produced it.
