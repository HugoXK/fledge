A superadmin can now preview and confirm deletion of one account from its support view. The preview is read-only and names the repositories, jobs, instances, tokens and GitHub installations the confirm will remove; the confirm needs a fresh GitHub confirmation and the exact login, refuses self-deletion and deleting the last superadmin, then cancels the account's jobs, stops and removes its instances, revokes its tokens and erases its rows through the same transaction the trial purge uses, leaving one record with no login, email, token or user id that carries the row counts and the per-currency spend.
