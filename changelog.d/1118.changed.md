A job's instruction contract now records the hash of the playbook text the team is told rather than the goal's fingerprint, so two `v8` playbooks that differ in the protocol, a role text or the team label are different records, and the repository page prints the first twelve characters of that hash under the goal form. The text is the one the instance applies — built from the repository goal, never from a job's own prompt, which for review, discover, triage, scope and verify and for any carry-over follow-up differs in the section the text calls authoritative. The sealed context bundle carries the same value; the rules commit hash stays on the job and its event.
