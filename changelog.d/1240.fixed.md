Three accessibility defects the new axe pass found are fixed. The chat launcher paired a light accent background with near-white text, about 1.5:1 in the dark palette, and now pairs the strong accent with that ink like every other button, so its label reads above 4.5:1 in both schemes. A link that sits inside a sentence is underlined instead of being marked by colour alone. The wordmark no longer carries an `aria-label` that disagrees with the text beside it, so assistive technology announces the name a sighted reader sees rather than a different one.
