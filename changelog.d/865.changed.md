The job page header now leads with one primary action, chosen by what a reader must do first: acknowledge an unacknowledged rule change, then execute an approved plan, then resume a paused job, then retry a failed one; a queued or running job shows none. Every other action keeps its form and action URL and moves as a ghost into one "More actions" disclosure, while Pause and Cancel stay visible because a stop control must never hide behind "more". The work-type, why-this-change, postmortem and team-participation cards collapse on demand, so the result, evidence, verification and timeline sit closer to the header; each folded card keeps its markup and attributes.
