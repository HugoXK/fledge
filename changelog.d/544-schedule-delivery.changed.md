The schedule console now reports what actually happened to a run's notifications instead of the fixed `unknown`. A notification a job queued carries that job's id, so the console reads the outbox for the newest decided instant and names the state: sent, queued, sending, retry scheduled, failed or blocked. A run that queued no external notification reads `none`, a send that may have crossed the boundary reads `unconfirmed` and is never resent automatically, and `unknown` now means only that nothing was read or nothing has run.
