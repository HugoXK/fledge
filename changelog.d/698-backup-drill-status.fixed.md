The admin backup card no longer repeats every legacy or size-only copy: it lists only a copy that contradicts its manifest or whose manifest cannot be read, names copies without a manifest in one clause, and counts encrypted copies beside the plaintext ones. The restore drill now records its result in `${DATA_DIR}/backup-drill.json` (or the path passed with `--status-file`), so the card shows the last real run's time, `ok`/`failed` and RTO/RPO instead of a fixed "none recorded", and a failed drill overwrites the previous success.
