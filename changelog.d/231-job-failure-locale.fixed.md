A failure now carries a stable code and the sentence is built from it in the reader's language — credential, stall, lease, timeout, quota, budget, duplicate-work, stage-evidence, bridge and disabled-repository failures all read as one sentence in English or Chinese. A refused enqueue reads the same way, on every button that starts a job: a full queue, the per-user queue limit, the monthly repository budget, a revoked credential, a missing or malformed target branch, an already-running job, a disabled review role and a refusal to resume or cancel now say what stopped them in the reader's language instead of repeating the backend's Chinese.
