The next-step decision on a finished verify or review job is now accepted from the same role the job page uses to draw the form. A repository connected before its upstream access was checked keeps `repo_role` NULL; its owner saw the decision form and had the write refused with "choose your role first". The route now decides by the actor's role, so the page and the write answer one question, and an actor who may not decide is still told which role the decision needs.
