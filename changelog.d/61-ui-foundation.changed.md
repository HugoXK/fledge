The console now has a design-token layer, an app shell that stays usable at 360, 768 and 1280, and one shared component for the loading, empty, error, success and disabled states. Tables become labelled cards on a phone, the page action sticks to the bottom where a thumb reaches it, every tappable control is at least 44px tall, and streaming areas announce updates through `aria-live`/`aria-busy` instead of changing silently. Colours are computed for WCAG AA contrast, the active navigation item carries `aria-current`, and inline layout styles are gone from the templates.
