The admin automation card now names the oldest live job lease (job, kind, repository, age and expiry) and the newest reaped attempt, both read from the durable lease rows rather than the process counters. `/metrics` adds `fledge_job_lease_age_seconds` (absent when no lease is live) and `fledge_job_lease_renew_failures_total{kind}` for a live renewal refused because the lease moved, beside the existing `fledge_lease_expired_total{kind}` for the reaper's terminal attempt, and `FledgeLeaseReaped` pages when that counter increases within 15 minutes.
